# Content: Audience & Scenarios

### Plain-language, non-technical audience (~B1 reading level)
Content is for local-government comms staff with no programming background. Write at roughly B1 reading level in both EN and PL. Avoid CLI and developer jargon. Introduce domain terms (project, skill, MCP) explicitly the first time they appear — never assume the reader knows what they mean.

### Municipal comms scenarios only — no toy examples
Every exercise must be rooted in real Gmina Kosakowo / local-council communications tasks: Facebook posts, resident FAQs, multi-source briefings, proofreading, presentation outlines. Generic "summarize this text" or programming-style exercises do not belong here.

**Why this matters**: The audience will close the tab the moment they hit jargon or feel the exercises don't map to their actual work. Content discipline is what makes the app useful.
